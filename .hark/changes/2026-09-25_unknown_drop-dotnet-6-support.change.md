---
title: ⚠️ Drop support for .NET 6
semver_level: major
released_in_version: 53.0.0
---

Per our [language runtime support policy](https://docs.stripe.com/sdks/versioning#language-policies), Stripe.net now requires .NET Core 8 or later. .NET Standard 2.0 and .NET Framework 4.6.2 remain supported.
